1

You now have a
"Second Gatekeeper"

What's happening:

Google remains the primary driver of web traffic, but now there's a second layer deciding what people see first: AI answers.

What it means for marketers:

Not all research leads to a visit anymore. But the visits that happen are higher intent. AI answers filter out casual browsers, so clicks come from users who are comparing options, evaluating brands, and are ready to convert.

💫 Do this next:
  • Track better metrics: Focus on lead quality, conversions, and engaged visits. Not just raw traffic.
  • Optimize for two worlds: Make sure your brand shows up in search results + AI answers (not just one).

2

AI may use your
content, but not
mention your brand

What's happening:

In many industries, 90%+ of AI-cited pages don't get real brand credit or traffic.

What it means for marketers:

You're doing the work, but don't get the clicks.

💫 Do this next:
  • Start tracking AI visibility: Track where your brand shows up inside AI tools, not just on Google.
    (Semrush One and Semrush AIO can help here)
  • Create content that makes it easier for AI to cite you by name.
